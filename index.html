Please wear headphones

Laptop and phone speakers hide the differences this study is about, and ratings made on them are not usable. If you do not have headphones to hand, please come back when you do rather than continuing on speakers.

You will hear short clips of everyday sounds. For each one give two ratings: how clean it sounds, and how well it matches the name we give it. About 15 minutes. Anonymous, and used only in aggregate for a research paper.

Sound quality is about the recording itself — free of fuzziness, distortion, hiss, or a muffled, smeared quality. A clean recording of the wrong thing still scores high here. Category fit is whether you would call it by that name. The number of events does not matter; one footstep and twenty can both fit perfectly.

Find somewhere quiet, set a comfortable volume, and keep it there for the whole session — changing it part way makes your ratings inconsistent with each other.

Voluntary, and you may close the page at any time — nothing is recorded until you press submit. We store your ratings, browser type and screen size. No name, email or IP address.
